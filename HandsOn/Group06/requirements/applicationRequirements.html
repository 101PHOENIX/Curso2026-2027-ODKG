Purpose Requirements Screen concepts

01 · The application

From a citywide CSV to a useful local view

LA 311 Fieldnotes lets people explore the City of Los Angeles' MyLA311 Service Request Data 2025. A visitor can look up a service request, browse reported issues around an address, or compare the mix of requests across city areas.

The application treats each row as a service-request record, not as a confirmed diagnosis of a problem or a live account of city operations. It brings together the record's request type, status, creation date, location, reporting channel and assignment fields.

Dataset at a glance

MyLA311 Service Request Data 2025 is published by the City of Los Angeles Information Technology Agency. The repository uses a 2025 CSV extract of the portal table.

Open data · CC0 1.0 · City of Los Angeles

Residents

Check whether a request is already recorded nearby and read its published status.

Neighborhood groups

Get a practical picture of request types reported in a council area or ZIP code.

City service and operations teams

311 coordinators and staff in departments such as sanitation or street maintenance can review assigned requests and plan follow-up or field work.

Researchers & journalists

Ask comparable questions across dates, request categories and city areas.

02 · What people can do

Application requirements

  1. FR1

    Show located requests on a map with different icons by request type; allow users to switch to a density view.

  2. FR2

    Search for a request by its service-request number or address, and filter by type, status, date and city area.

  3. FR3

    Open a request to see its published type, status, creation date, location, reporting source and assignment fields when available.

  4. FR4

    Summarize request counts by type and status for a selected date range and area.

  5. FR5

    Display the dataset source, 2025 extract period and CC0 license.

03 · Quality and limits

How it should behave

Non-functional requirements

  • Work in a standard browser and adapt to mobile screens.
  • Query the knowledge graph through SPARQL and use standard location and date representations.
  • Keep status legible without relying on color alone, and show source and license attribution.

Data limits

  • The app explores the 2025 CSV extract; it is not a live 311 tracker or a request-submission tool.
