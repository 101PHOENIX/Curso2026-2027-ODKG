Dataset Requirements

Selected dataset

Historical Occupancy of Public Municipal Parking in Madrid. The dataset contains historical information about the occupancy of public parking facilities in Madrid.

R1. Smart Cities Domain

Satisfied. The dataset is related to urban mobility and parking management, which are important areas of smart cities.

R2. CSV Format

Satisfied. The dataset is available in CSV format and can therefore be used for the RDF transformation required in the course.

R3. Open License

Satisfied. The dataset is published under the CC BY 4.0 license, allowing reuse and redistribution with appropriate attribution.

R4. Linkable to Real-World Entities

Satisfied. The dataset contains information about real parking facilities in Madrid, including their names and districts. These entities can be linked to geographic information such as streets, addresses, districts and coordinates.

R5. Documentation

Satisfied. The Madrid Open Data Portal provides information and documentation about the dataset and its fields, making the data easier to understand and reuse.
