Dataset Requirements Analysis

Group: Group 03

Primary Datasets: Contaminación acústica. Datos diarios (215885, daily measurements) & Contaminación acústica: Estaciones de medida (211346, monitoring stations)

Portal: datos.madrid.es (Ayuntamiento de Madrid)

R1. Smart Cities Domain Mandatory

Satisfied

The datasets monitor environmental noise pollution (acoustic quality) collected by urban sensor networks throughout the city of Madrid. Noise management, acoustic zoning, and monitoring citizen environmental health are essential pillars of modern smart city governance.

R2. Available as CSV Mandatory

Satisfied

Both datasets are published on the portal in CSV format. Our copies are in the csv/ folder:

  • Measurements: csv/contaminacion_acustica.csv (extract of the portal file 215885-0-contaminacion-ruido.csv, rows from 2020 on) — contains columns: Estación;Año;Mes;Día;Periodo;LAeq;L1;L10;L50;L90;L99. The Periodo field categorizes readings into D (daytime: 7-19h), E (evening: 19-23h), N (nighttime: 23-7h), and T (24-hour total).
  • Stations Catalog: csv/estaciones_acusticas.csv (full copy of the portal file 211346-4-estaciones-acusticas-csv.csv, 31 stations) — contains columns: ESTACIÓN;NOMBRE;UBICACIÓN;DISTRITO;BARRIO;LONGITUD;LATITUD;X;Y;ALTITUD;FECHA ALTA (station ID, name, street address, district, neighborhood, geographic and UTM coordinates, altitude and start date).
R3. Open License for Republication Mandatory

Satisfied

The datasets are released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) license via datos.madrid.es. This permits copying, distribution, transformation, and online publishing for educational and commercial purposes with attribution.

R4. Linkable to Real-World Entities Mandatory

Satisfied

The station catalog enables direct linking to external knowledge graphs (Wikidata, DBpedia, GeoNames):

  • Geographical Entities: Station records contain district and neighborhood names (e.g., Centro → Wikidata wd:Q1763376, Justicia → Wikidata wd:Q1992319, and the city itself Madrid → Wikidata wd:Q2807).
  • Spatial Coordinates: UTM coordinates (X and Y columns) and geographic coordinates permit geospatial linking (WGS84 / GeoSPARQL).
  • Semantic Ontologies: Noise sensor readings map cleanly to standard ontologies such as SOSA/SSN (Sensor, Observation, Sample, and Actuation) and QUDT (Quantities, Units, Dimensions, and Types) using decibels (dB(A)).
R5. Documentation Exists Optional

Partially Satisfied

Official technical documentation (PDF format) is provided on the portal defining the 11 schema columns and statistical percentiles (L1, L10, L50, L90, L99, LAeq). The time interval definitions for periods D, E, N, and T are standardized under Spanish acoustic regulation (Real Decreto 1367/2007).

R6. Multiple Data Sources Optional

Satisfied

We integrate an additional open dataset from Madrid's data portal: Censo de locales, sus actividades y terrazas de hostelería y restauración (200085, census of premises, activities, and hospitality terraces). This dataset publishes four files (activities, premises, premises with licence information, and terraces). We only use the terraces file, which is the one needed for our application; the other three are not used.

  • Terraces: csv/terrazas_hosteleria.csv (full copy of the portal file 200085-6-censo-locales.csv, 6,594 terraces, 117 columns). It supplies the terrace identifier (id_terraza), UTM coordinates (coordenada_x_local, coordenada_y_local), district and neighborhood, authorized table and chair counts (mesas_es, sillas_es, mesas_ra, sillas_ra) and opening hours. Terraces will be cross-referenced with nighttime acoustic noise (Periodo = N).

Data Preprocessing & Cleansing Steps

  • Identifier Reconciliation: Daily measurements identify stations via numeric IDs (2, 3...), while the station catalog uses labels like RF-02, RF-03. We map {n} → RF-{n}, with {n} padded to two digits (2 → RF-02, 86 → RF-86), during preprocessing.
  • Coordinate Correction: Geographic coordinates in the catalog use period separators erroneously (e.g., -3.691.877 instead of -3.691877). Repair the observed geographic format and check it against ETRS89 / UTM 30N (EPSG:25830) X/Y transformed to WGS84. Terrace locations use valid local or grouping coordinates according to access type; the precise selection rules and limitations are documented in analysis.html.
  • Missing Values & Numbers: Assertions for missing entries flagged as N/D are omitted. European decimal commas (68,9) become decimal dots in typed RDF literals ("68.9"^^xsd:decimal).
  • Subset Selection: Because the raw daily noise history spans from 1998 to present (about 45 MB, over one million rows), our repository includes an extract (csv/contaminacion_acustica.csv) with all rows from 2020 on (01/01/2020 to the last day published, 295,853 rows), kept in the original encoding and format.
