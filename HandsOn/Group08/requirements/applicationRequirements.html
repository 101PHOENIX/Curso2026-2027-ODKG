Application Requirements

Hands-on 1 · Open Data and Knowledge Graphs · Group 08 · Barcelona Heat Relief

1. Purpose

A web application on top of a knowledge graph that lets users find and explore the public resources that Barcelona offers to cope with heat: climate shelters, drinking fountains and street trees. The application shows them on a map, lists them in a catalogue with filters and summarises each neighbourhood. Neighbourhoods and tree species are enriched with data from Wikidata.

2. Users and use cases

UserNeed
Resident or visitorFind climate shelters and drinking fountains in a neighbourhood and know what kind of place each shelter is.
Urban planner or analystCompare neighbourhoods and districts by number and type of resources and by tree species.
Curious citizenDiscover which tree species grow in a neighbourhood and read about them.

3. Data used

DatasetRole in the application
D1 - Climate shelters networkMap layer and catalogue of shelters, filterable by category (derived from the name) and neighbourhood.
D2 - Drinking fountainsMap layer and catalogue of fountains.
D3 - Street treesMap layer, species catalogue and counts per neighbourhood.
WikidataDescriptions and images of neighbourhoods, districts, species and well-known facilities.

5. Questions the knowledge graph must answer

  1. Which climate shelters are in a given neighbourhood and of what category are they?
  2. How many drinking fountains does each district have?
  3. Which are the most frequent tree species in a given neighbourhood?
  4. Which neighbourhoods have no shelter of a given category (for example no library)?
  5. How many trees were planted since a given year in each district?
  6. Which resources (shelters, fountains) and how many trees are in a given neighbourhood?
  7. Which shelters correspond to a facility that has a Wikidata item?
  8. Which species of a neighbourhood are linked to Wikidata and what are their common names?

7. Non-functional requirements and architecture

  • The application queries a SPARQL endpoint that exposes the generated knowledge graph; it does not read the CSV files directly.
  • Web application with an interactive map. The technology will be decided in the next assignments.
  • Responsive layout usable on desktop and mobile screens.
  • Interface texts in English, with names of places and species as published in the datasets.
  • Attribution to Ajuntament de Barcelona (CC BY 4.0) and to Wikidata in the footer of the application.

8. Out of scope

  • Real-time information (opening hours, availability of shelters, water quality).
  • Route planning between resources.
