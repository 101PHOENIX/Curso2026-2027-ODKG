Application Requirements

  1. The app must enable the user to search for a train station by name and open its detail view.
  2. The app must enable the user to filter for several categories, e.g. the region, the train line or the category (regional, long-distance etc.)
  3. The app must enable the user to look at an overview of all stations on a map. In the overview colors must symbolize the average delay of trains for a specific station.
  4. The app must enable the user to look at a detail view of a station, where specific data related to it is shown. For example the daily number of trains, available train lines, on-time vs. delayed share etc.
  5. The app must enable the user to look at a list of the most and least reliable stations in a given region.
