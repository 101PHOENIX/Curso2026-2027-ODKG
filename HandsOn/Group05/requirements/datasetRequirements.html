Selected dataset

For this hands-on, we have selected the dataset Hourly air quality data since 2016, published by the Ajuntament de València through its VLCi open data platform: https://opendata.vlci.valencia.es/dataset/hourly-air-quality-data-since-2016

The resource used is rvvcca.-datos-horarios-valencia-2016-2021-curt-cas.csv. It contains hourly measurements recorded by the monitoring stations of the Red Valenciana de Vigilancia y Control de la Contaminación Atmosférica (RVVCCA) in the city of València between 2016 and 2021. The stations included are Av. França, Bulevard Sud, Molí del Sol, Pista Silla and Universitat Politècnica, and the measured parameters are SO2, CO, NO, NO2, NOx, O3, PM10 and PM2.5, together with wind speed, wind direction and maximum wind speed.

R1. Data are in the smart cities domain.

It belongs to the smart cities domain since it is oriented to urban air-quality monitoring.

R2. Data are available as a CSV file.

The dataset is published in CSV format, with separate resources for Spanish, Valencian , and English column labels. The resource used in this hands-on is rvvcca.-datos-horarios-valencia-2016-2021-curt-cas.csv, with mimetype text/csv, available from the dataset page linked above. The portal classifies the dataset as "3 estrellas" on the five-star open data scale, which corresponds to structured data published in an open format.

R3. Data have an open license so they can be published.

The dataset is published under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0), http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/deed.es.

R4. Data can be easily linked with generic real-world entities (e.g., locations).

The dataset contains several attributes that act as linking points to generic real-world entities. The table below identifies, for each attribute, the external entity it can be linked to and the target dataset or vocabulary.

Attribute in the selected dataset External entity Target dataset / vocabulary
Station name (Av. França, Bulevard Sud, Molí del Sol, Pista Silla, Universitat Politècnica) Named place or institution in the city of València Wikidata, esDBpedia
Station name, taken as a sensing platform Monitoring station / sensor SOSA/SSN (sosa:Platform, sosa:Sensor)
City of València (implicit in the whole dataset) Municipality Wikidata (Q8818), esDBpedia, esadm:Municipio
Pollutant codes (SO2, CO, NO, NO2, NOx, O3, PM10, PM2.5) Chemical substance / observable property SOSA/SSN (sosa:ObservableProperty), EEA air quality vocabularies
Date and hour of each reading Instant or interval in time OWL-Time (time:Instant, time:Interval)
Measurement units Unit of measure om:Unit_of_measure
Meteorological columns (wind speed, wind direction, maximum wind speed) Weather observation SOSA/SSN, AEMET open data

The station name is the main linking attribute, since the dataset identifies each monitoring station by name and those stations correspond to real places in the city. Note that the CSV does not include the geographic coordinates of the stations, so the link to a geographic point has to be established through the station name rather than directly from a coordinate pair.

Entities in other datasets that can be linked with the selected dataset

Linking also works in the opposite direction. The following entities, which exist in other datasets, can be linked back to resources in our dataset and enrich them with information that the original CSV does not contain:

  • The city of València in Wikidata (Q8818) and in esDBpedia provides population figures, surface area, geographic boundaries, and climate information which allow the measurements to be interpreted in context.
  • Districts and neighbourhoods of València published in Wikidata and esDBpedia carry population and area data, making it possible to relate pollutant concentrations to population density in the area surrounding each station.
  • Universitat Politècnica de València is a real-world institution present in Wikidata and DBpedia, and one of the monitoring stations is located on its campus, so the station can be linked to the institution.
  • Air quality limit values defined by EU Directive 2008/50/EC and the recommended thresholds published by the World Health Organization can be linked to the pollutant properties, enabling the application to flag readings that exceed them.
  • Meteorological observations published by AEMET for the same period and geographic area can be linked through time and location, allowing analysis of the relationship between weather conditions and pollutant concentrations.
  • Station location data published in the per-station datasets of the same portal can be linked through the station name, supplying the coordinates that the hourly dataset does not provide.

R5. Documentation exists for the data (optional).

The Valencia hourly air quality dataset has supporting documentation available through the official València Open Data portal. The documentation provides a general description of the dataset, information about the monitored stations and measured parameters, metadata such as the CSV format and CC BY 4.0 licence, and a data dictionary explaining the available columns. However, the documentation is relatively limited, as it does not provide detailed information about measurement units, missing values, quality-control procedures, or the precise geographic coordinates of the monitoring stations.

R6. Data can come from multiple data sources (optional).

The Valencia dataset is published by the Ajuntament de València, and the portal identifies its source as the Red Valenciana de Vigilancia y Control de la Contaminación Atmosférica. The CSV combines observations from multiple monitoring stations, but these are stations within the same monitoring network/city rather than genuinely separate data sources.
