3. D1 – Air quality. Daily data 2024

R1. Smart city domain ✔

The dataset belongs to the urban environment domain. It contains the daily measurements of air pollutants recorded by the automatic air quality monitoring network of Madrid City Council, a network of urban sensors typical of a smart city. Each daily value is the arithmetic mean of the 24 hourly values of that day.

R2. Available as CSV ✔

The portal publishes the dataset in CSV, TXT and XML, with one file per year since 2001. We selected 2024, which has 1,410 rows.

  • Field separator ; and decimal separator ..
  • Wide format: each row corresponds to one station × pollutant × month. Columns D01…D31 hold the daily values and V01…V31 their validity flags (V = valid, N = not valid).
  • Before the RDF transformation, the file will need to be reshaped into a long format, with one observation per station, pollutant and day.

R3. Open license ✔

Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), as stated in the dataset page and in the portal's terms of use. The license allows the data to be copied, transformed and redistributed, including for commercial purposes. The only condition is to credit the source (Madrid City Council).

D1 alone does not contain the geographic location of the measurements. The location is obtained by linking to D2 (see section 6).

R5. Documentation ✔ High degree

The dataset page provides a PDF on how to interpret the data. It describes the file structure, the station, pollutant and sampling technique codes, the validity flags and the units (µg/m³, except CO in mg/m³). This is enough to interpret every column unambiguously. The only missing piece is a machine-readable data dictionary.

R6. Multiple data sources ◐

D1 is a single source on its own. It becomes part of a multi-source knowledge graph once linked to D2, D3 and Wikidata. See section 7 for the discussion.

4. D2 – Air quality. Monitoring stations

R1. Smart city domain ✔

The dataset describes the city's environmental sensing infrastructure: the 24 stations of Madrid's air quality monitoring network, with their location, type and the pollutants each one measures.

R2. Available as CSV ✔

Available as CSV (24 rows, 25 columns) and in other geographic formats.

  • Separator ;, UTF-8 encoding with BOM.
  • Coordinates are given in three formats: degrees-minutes-seconds (LONGITUD_ETRS89, LATITUD_ETRS89), UTM ETRS89 with a decimal comma (COORDENADA_X/Y_ETRS89) and decimal degrees (LONGITUD, LATITUD).
  • Stations 49 and 59 use a different degrees-minutes-seconds notation from the rest, so we will use the decimal-degree columns.

R3. Open license ✔

CC BY 4.0, with the same conditions as D1.

R5. Documentation ✔ High degree

The dataset page includes the PDF “Calidad del aire. Estaciones de control. Contenido y estructura del fichero”, which describes every field.

R6. Multiple data sources ◐

D2 is the bridge between the measurements (D1) and the rest of the graph. Its coordinates allow it to be linked to the sports facilities (D3), the districts and GeoNames, and its place names to Wikidata.

5. D3 – Sports. Basic municipal sports facilities

R1. Smart city domain ✔

The dataset belongs to the urban public services and facilities domain. It is the city's inventory of basic municipal sports facilities, mostly open-air: football pitches, futsal courts, skating rinks, calisthenics parks, fitness trails, pétanque courts, BMX tracks and so on. For each facility it gives the location, public transport, equipment and accessibility.

R2. Available as CSV ✔

Available as CSV (607 facilities, 32 columns).

  • Separator ;, all fields enclosed in double quotes.
  • ISO-8859-1 (Latin-1) encoding, unlike D1 and D2, so it must be converted to UTF-8 before the RDF transformation.
  • The facility type is encoded as a path in TIPO (e.g. /contenido/entidadesYorganismos/CentrosEspaciosDeporte/PistaFutbolSala). Its last segment gives the category.
  • Several columns are almost always empty: HORARIO has 9 values, TELEFONO 14, and FAX and EMAIL none.

R3. Open license ✔

CC BY 4.0, as stated in the dataset page. Same conditions as D1 and D2.

R5. Documentation ✔ High degree

The dataset page includes the PDF “Equipamientos. Contenido y estructura del fichero”, which describes the fields shared by all of the council's facility datasets. The coding of the ACCESIBILIDAD field (values 0–3) should be checked against it.

R6. Multiple data sources ◐

D3 comes from a different area of the council (Sports) than D1 and D2 (Environment). It provides the administrative geography (districts, neighbourhoods, postal codes) that the air quality datasets lack. See section 7.

6. How the datasets are linked

D1 ↔ D2 (key join): D1.ESTACION = D2.CODIGO_CORTO, or equivalently the prefix of D1.PUNTO_MUESTREO (28079004_6_48) = D2.CODIGO. All 24 stations present in D1 also appear in D2.

D2 ↔ D3 (spatial join): the two datasets share no key, but both have WGS84 coordinates. Each sports facility is assigned its nearest monitoring station by distance, which lets the air quality measurements be attached to the facility.

D3 → districts → D2: the district and neighbourhood codes in D3 act as the administrative reference to which the stations can also be assigned.

All → Wikidata: municipality, districts, pollutants, sports and landmark places.

7. Discussion of the optional requirements

R5 – High. All three datasets have official PDF documentation describing their structure and codes. What is missing is machine-readable metadata (e.g. CSVW), and a few codes (ACCESIBILIDAD) need to be checked against the PDFs.

R6 – Medium–high. The graph combines three datasets from two different areas of the council (Environment and Sports) with an external linked data source (Wikidata). It is not higher because all three CSV files share the same publisher (Madrid City Council) and portal. To raise it further we could add:

  • the stations of the Air Quality Network of the Community of Madrid (CSV, different publisher);
  • the council's daily weather data (available since 2019), to relate outdoor activity conditions to wind and rain.

8. Conclusion

All three datasets satisfy the mandatory requirements R1–R4. D1 satisfies R4 through its link to D2. D3 is the strongest dataset for R4 thanks to its districts, neighbourhoods, postal codes, addresses and metro stations. Of the optional requirements, R5 is satisfied to a high degree and R6 to a medium–high degree.
